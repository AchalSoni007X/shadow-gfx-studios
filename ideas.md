# SHADOW GFX STUDIOS — Design Direction

## Three Possible Directions

### Theme Name: Printed Signal
Very high-contrast studio portfolio that treats the website like a contemporary design zine: white paper, ink-black typography, and electric purple registration marks. It feels direct, editorial, and unmistakably made by a graphic designer.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Afterimage Arcade
A dark, kinetic portfolio with restrained ultraviolet glow, scanline texture, and motion inspired by pixel stretching and nightlife posters. It feels experimental and energetic without losing a clear portfolio structure.

**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Quiet Geometry
A calmer gallery experience with warm white space, sharp black rules, and purple geometry used as a precise accent. It feels premium, composed, and more like a boutique design practice than a personal portfolio.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Direction: Printed Signal

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design with references to Swiss poster systems, risograph print registration, and Indian independent design studios. The site should feel like an art-directed portfolio spread rather than a generic landing page.

### Core Principles
1. **Ink before decoration:** black type and white space carry the hierarchy; purple is an intentional signal, never a gradient wash.
2. **Asymmetric composition:** use split columns, offset captions, and edge-anchored details instead of stacking every section into centered cards.
3. **Work as evidence:** the supplied automotive edits, flyer, and portfolio examples are the hero content; copy supports the visuals rather than competing with them.
4. **Controlled motion:** hover reveals, marquee text, and audio-reactive details should feel like print registration shifting by a few millimetres—not like a template full of effects.

### Color Philosophy
The base is paper white (#F7F6F2) and soft ink (#111014), making the site feel tactile and legible. Signature purple (#6D28D9) is the studio’s registration ink: vivid enough to identify the brand, but used in blocks, rules, labels, and hover states rather than foggy gradients. A deep plum-black (#1D102D) gives the featured work a stage without turning the whole page into a dark theme.

### Layout Paradigm
A long editorial scroll with a narrow left rail for section numbering and a wide right canvas for work. The hero uses a two-column composition: a sharp typographic statement on the left and a full-bleed image stack on the right. Services, approach, and contact are arranged as poster-like bands with clipped corners and oversized numerals.

### Signature Elements
- A purple “SG” monogram stamp used as a compact navigation mark and favicon concept.
- Registration ticks, thin black rules, and tiny uppercase index labels inspired by print production notes.
- Pixel-stretch hover treatment on artwork captions: image movement is subtle while the label briefly offsets in purple and black.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction should reveal craft. Navigation anchors scroll to sections; project tiles open a focused lightbox with title and medium; the audio control is explicit and user-controlled because browsers block autoplay. Buttons should feel like printed labels that invert to purple ink on hover.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out transitions for hover states and a slow 12–18s drift for decorative image layers. On entry, stagger the hero eyebrow, headline, and CTA by 50ms intervals. Keep image hover transforms under 2.5% scale and use opacity/transform only. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing marquee and drift animation while preserving clarity.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for display headlines and **DM Mono** for labels, metadata, and navigation. Headlines are large, tight, and slightly irregular in line breaks; body copy is short and measured. Labels use uppercase with generous tracking. Avoid Inter and default UI typography.

### Brand Essence
**Positioning:** SHADOW GFX STUDIOS is Achal Soni’s independent graphic design practice for brands, creators, and local businesses that need visual work with speed, personality, and a professional finish.

**Personality:** Precise. Electric. Approachable.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative and visual. CTAs are direct invitations, never vague conversion language. Microcopy sounds like a designer speaking plainly to a client.

- “Make the first impression impossible to scroll past.”
- “Bring the brief. Leave with a sharper identity.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a custom-style “SG” monogram built from two interlocking, squared-off letterforms: the S is purple and the G is black, both contained in an open rectangular stamp. Pair it with a two-line wordmark where “SHADOW GFX” is uppercase and “STUDIOS” sits below with tighter tracking.

### Signature Brand Color
**Registration Purple — #6D28D9.** It is the unmistakable ink mark that identifies the studio across rules, stamps, labels, and calls to action.
